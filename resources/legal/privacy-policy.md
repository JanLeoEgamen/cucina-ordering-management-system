This policy explains what personal information {app_name} collects when you create an account and place orders, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Your account:** your first and last name, your company (chosen from the list our staff keep), your email address and a password. The password is stored scrambled (hashed); nobody, including our staff, can read it.
- **Google sign-in:** if you sign in with Google we receive your name, your email address and your Google account ID. We never see your Google password.
- **Your orders:** what you ordered and when, the order number, the total, the payment method you chose and the status of the order and its payment.
- **Proof of payment:** if you pay by bank transfer or e-wallet, the screenshot or photo you upload to prove it.
- **Notifications:** the messages we send you about your orders, and whether you have read them.
- **Activity records:** to keep the service safe and to settle disputes, we record who did what and when (for example signing in, placing or cancelling an order), together with the IP address and the browser used.

## How we use it

- To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
- To take, prepare, hand over and, if needed, cancel your orders.
- To check that a payment was made.
- To tell you about your orders (in the app, and by email or Google Chat when we have switched that on).
- To protect the service against misuse and to investigate problems.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who can see it

- **Our staff** can see the orders and payments they need to do their work. What each person can see depends on their role.
- **Service providers** that make the service work for us: the hosting of this website, the email service that delivers our messages, and Google when you sign in with Google or receive our Google Chat messages. They only get what they need for that purpose.
- **Anyone else** only if the law requires us to share it.

## Cookies and what your browser keeps

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in and a security cookie that protects forms from forgery. Your browser also remembers your light or dark theme and the layout of the menu. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and your orders for as long as your account exists and as long as we need them to run the business and keep our records. Activity records are deleted automatically after a limited time. When an account is deleted, it can no longer be used to sign in.

## Your choices

- You can see and correct your name, company and email address under **Profile**, and change your password there.
- You can delete your account from **Profile**.
- You can ask us to correct, give you a copy of, or erase the information we hold about you by contacting us (see below). We may have to keep some order and payment records where we are required to.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
