## Ordering

- Food is sold in **ordering sessions**: a time window with a limited stock of each dish. What you see on the menu is what is available right now.
- Adding a dish to your cart does not hold it. Stock is taken when you place the order. If a dish runs out while you are ordering, we will tell you and you can change your order.
- **Pre-orders** are for big groups and events. They are placed from the pre-order menu while a pre-order session is open, and each order must hold at least the minimum number of plates shown there.
- You are responsible for the accuracy of what you order. Please check your order before you place it.

## Prices and payment

- Prices are shown in pesos and are the prices at the time you place the order.
- You can pay **cash on pick up**, or pay first by **bank transfer or e-wallet** and upload a proof of payment.
- A cash order is paid at the counter when you collect it.
- A bank or e-wallet payment is checked by our staff before your order is handed over. If the proof is rejected you can send a new one, or switch to cash on pick up. Your plates stay held for you while this is sorted out.
- An order is only handed over once its payment is settled.

## Pick up

- Orders are collected from us. We will tell you in the app when your order is ready.
- Please collect your order during or soon after the session.
- Please keep your order number: you may be asked to show it.

## Cancelling an order

- You can cancel your own order only while it is still pending, and only when we allow it. If you do not see a Cancel button on your order, please contact the staff.
- Once your payment has been verified, only our staff can cancel the order.
- When an order is cancelled, its dishes go back on the menu for others.
- If you have already paid and need your money back, please ask the staff.

## Your account

- Give us true information and keep your password to yourself. You are responsible for what is done with your account.
- Do not misuse the service: do not place orders you do not intend to collect, do not upload proofs of payment that are not real, and do not try to get around the limits on stock and quantity.
- We may suspend or delete an account that breaks these terms.

## Changes

We may change these terms, the menu, the prices and the way we take orders at any time. The date at the top of this page shows when the terms last changed. Your use of the service after a change means you accept it.
