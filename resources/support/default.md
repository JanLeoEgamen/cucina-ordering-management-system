## Ordering

**How do I order?** Sign in (or create an account), open **Menu** under **Ordering**, add the dishes you want and place your order. The menu shows the dishes of the ordering session that is open right now. When no session is open there is nothing to order yet, and the home page tells you when the next one starts.

**A dish is sold out.** Each session has a limited stock of every dish. Once it is gone it is gone: check the menu again later, or in the next session.

**I need food for a group or an event.** Use the pre-order menu while a pre-order session is open. Each pre-order must hold a minimum number of plates, which is shown there.

## Paying

**How can I pay?** Pay cash when you pick up your order, or pay first by bank transfer or e-wallet and attach your proof of payment. The ways we accept are shown when you place your order.

**When is my payment checked?** A cash payment is settled when you collect your order. A bank or e-wallet payment is checked by our staff before your order is handed over.

**My payment was rejected.** Your order is still held for you. Open **My Orders**, then send a new proof of payment, or choose to pay with cash when you pick it up.

## My orders

**Where is my order?** **My Orders** shows what you ordered, its status and where your payment stands. You also get a notification (the bell at the top right) when your order is ready for pick up.

**Can I cancel an order?** While your order is still pending and we allow it, **My Orders** shows a **Cancel order** button. If you do not see it, or your payment has already been verified, please ask the staff.

## My account

**I forgot my password.** On the log in page choose **Forgot your password?** and follow the steps we send to your email address.

**How do I change my name, company, email or password?** Open **Profile** in the account menu at the top right.

**How do I delete my account?** At the bottom of **Profile** choose **Delete Account** and confirm with your password. You will be signed out and the account can no longer be used. Its details and your past orders are kept, and the same email address cannot be used to register again. While you have a pending order the account cannot be deleted: wait until it is completed or cancelled.
